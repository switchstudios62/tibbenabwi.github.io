What is Tibb-e-Nabwi

Guidance rooted in the Sunnah.

Tibb-e-Nabwi, or Prophetic Medicine, refers to the health and healing guidance found in the teachings of the Prophet Muhammad (Peace Be Upon Him).

لِكُلِّ دَاءِ دَوَاءٌ فَإِذَا أُصِيبَ دَوَاءُ الدَّاءِ بَرَأَ بِإِذْنِ اللَّهِ

“There is a medicine for every disease, and when the medicine is applied to the disease it is cured by God's permission.”

Hadith #2204 · Sahih Muslim

This timeless tradition offers remedies, practices, and lifestyle habits that promote health, balance, and harmony.
