What is Tibb-e-Nabwi

Guidance rooted in the Sunnah

Tibb-e-Nabwi, or Prophetic Medicine, refers to the health and healing guidance found in the teachings of the Prophet Muhammad (Peace Be Upon Him).

لِكُلِّ دَاءِ دَوَاءٌ فَإِذَا أُصِيبَ دَوَاءُ الدَّاءَ بَرَأَ بِإِذْنِ

"There is a medicine for every disease, and when the medicine is applied to the disease it is cured by God's permission."

Hadith # 2204 · Sahih Muslim

This timeless tradition offers remedies, practices, and lifestyle habits that promote health, balance, and harmony, making it relevant for both personal wellness and modern healthcare.

About Us

About our founder

Azmat Aapa is a leading practitioner of Tibb-e Nabwi (Prophetic Medicine) and the founder of Nature's Choice, offering over 90 natural remedies since 2020. She also established Quran for Kids and the Institute of Tibb-e-Nabwi Medicine, promoting faith-based and holistic healing.

She has taught Tibb-e Nabwi, authored two books, and developed remedies like black seed oil tonics, Senna Makki teas, honey-based salves, blending Sunnah wisdom with modern health needs.

Traveling across Pakistan and Canada, Azmat Aapa delivers lectures, conducts clinics, and performs Hijama (cupping therapy), emphasizing preventive care and holistic wellness.

Her work revives Prophetic Medicine as a modern, practical approach to achieving physical, emotional, and spiritual health.
